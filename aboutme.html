About me

I am a theoretical and computational astrophysicist working on star and planet formation. My research combines analytical modelling, hydrodynamical (SPH) simulations, the interpretation of high-resolution ALMA observations and Bayesian methods, with the goal of understanding the physical processes that drive planet formation in the earliest phases of protostellar evolution, and how the natal environment shapes the diversity of planetary systems.

I developed the analytical GI Wiggle, a kinematic diagnostic of gravitational instability now widely used in observations, and contributed to rotation curve models that allow us to weigh protoplanetary discs. Within the exoALMA ALMA Large Programme I led the disc mass work package, delivering dynamical masses for 15 protoplanetary discs.
