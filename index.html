Homepage

Hi! I am a postdoctoral associate at the Institute of Astronomy, University of Cambridge. My research focusses on protoplanetary discs and planet formation, under a theoretical perspective. I am particularly interested in young protoplanetary discs, investigating the role of self gravity and gravitational instability in the planet formation process.
